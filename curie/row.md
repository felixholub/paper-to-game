| **Curie's Counterfactuals** | Skłodowska Curie (1898), *Comptes rendus* 126:1101–1103. Rays emitted by the compounds of uranium and thorium, as a ligne claire album: push back on the electrometer's spot of light with the quartz and write down what you measure, well or badly. Every number plants an idea and draws one dated scene of Paris, so your world grows as you play; at the end your ideas become a physics and your strip is shown whole with the world a century on, what life would be like if your note were true (ten worlds: a slow chemical fire, a wind from space, a crystal lens, an atom with no bottom, a world that leaks, an electric bone, a quiet metal, an Académie of doubt, a discovery made elsewhere, or hers), beside ours. | [play](https://steffenhuck.github.io/paper-to-game/curie/curies-counterfactuals.html) · [memo](curie/memo.md) |
