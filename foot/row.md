| **Foot's Bad Man** | Foot (1967), *Oxford Review* 5:5–15. The problem of abortion and the doctrine of the double effect, the paper in which the runaway tram first appears, as a silent-film melodrama in seven scenes. A bad man asks you to do seven things, each a worse deed than the last — turn a wheel, keep a bottle, brew a cure, shut a door, pull a trigger, take up the irons, light a fuse — to save a few people from him. You decide each one. After each, the house reads it as Foot reads it: harm against harm, help against help, or harm against help; which rule decides; and whether the famous rule about aiming agrees. Her line does not climb with the deeds: it crosses at the brew and comes back at the cave. An epilogue plays the three abortion cases the paper was written for. | [play](https://steffenhuck.github.io/paper-to-game/foot/foots-bad-man.html) · [memo](foot/memo.md) |
