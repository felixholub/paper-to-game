| **Lovelace's Cards** | Menabrea (1842), *Sketch of the Analytical Engine*, translated with Notes by Ada Lovelace, *Scientific Memoirs* 3 (1843): 666–731. A puzzle loom: six orders dealt at random from a library (two easy, two medium, two hard), each to be woven with as few cards as possible. The machine counts as her Engine does: wheels are numbers that start at 0, Set cards put numbers on them, and a Go back and count down card takes 1 off and looks (her Operation 7), so on the hard looms a loop sits inside a loop. Then the same cards play a tune and do sums, the machine is asked to make something up, and her own Bernoulli chain runs.
