# Noether's Ball — note for the author

1. Revision 7 (1 Nov 2026): plain words throughout. Nine cards, each a small picture with its formula on screen, every symbol said in words, and a change; one answer, *it matters* or *it doesn't*; then the trip after the change beside the first, the part of the formula that decides the answer, and the formula's number before and after. Every change that doesn't matter wins a prize, shown on a graph. Card 7 shows the ball's energy catching a fake throw; card 8 shows the slide's energy zero on every slide; card 9 says why, and gives the rule the slide gets instead. Six red badges for the paper's lessons, three pink for the traps; no score, just the shelf and a sentence.

Look first at `screenshot-a-c1-answer.png` (a prize won: energy's two parts and their flat sum), `screenshot-a-c7-answer.png` (energy steady on the real throw, wobbling on the re-timed one), `screenshot-a-c8-answer.png` (the slide's energy: zero on the real slide and on a straight one) and `screenshot-a-end.png`.
3. Everything tunable is in the `CONFIG` block at the top of `mechanism.js`, the only copy of the mechanism: the six worlds' numbers, the finite changes shown, the thresholds. The cards' words and badges are in the same file. Edit, run `node calibration.js` (14 checks against the paper), then `python3 build.py`, which refuses to finish if the embedded mechanism differs from the file; `node session-test.js` plays the game through at phone width.
