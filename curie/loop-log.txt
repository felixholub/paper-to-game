AIM: Uranium makes the air carry electricity. Find what else does.

PANEL 1 · PURE URANIUM (careful) → YARD
  GAME: Pure uranium: your yardstick.
  💭 So! This is how much electricity pure uranium lets the air carry. Everything else gets measured against it.

PANEL 2 · LEAD (written at 0.5) → ACTIVE, spot still running left
  GAME: Lead: about half as much electricity as pure uranium.
  💭 Wow! The air carries electricity near lead, and there’s no uranium in it! Every heavy atom must do it, the heavier the more… as if something were raining down on them from space!

PANEL 3 · THIN THORIUM OXIDE (careful) → ACTIVE
  GAME: Thin thorium oxide: about as much electricity as pure uranium.
  💭 Look at that! Not a speck of uranium in it, and the air still carries electricity! Thorium does it too. The two heaviest atoms there are… maybe the table of elements ends where atoms start to leak?

PANEL 4 · WHITE PHOSPHORUS (careful) → STRONG
  GAME: White phosphorus: a little more electricity than pure uranium.
  💭 Good heavens! White phosphorus does it, and there’s no uranium in it! There is phosphorus in every bone… is the stuff of life electric?

PANEL 5 · PITCHBLENDE FROM SAXONY (careful) → HIGH
  GAME: Pitchblende from Saxony: three and a half times as much electricity as pure uranium.
  💭 Well, well! Only two-thirds uranium, and yet three and a half times as strong as pure uranium! It can’t just be the uranium in it: something heavier than uranium must be hiding in this rock. A bigger sail!

PANEL 6 · CHALCOLITE FROM A MINE (careful) → HIGH, spot still running right
  GAME: Chalcolite from a mine: twice as much electricity as pure uranium.
  💭 Didn’t I know it! Another rock, and again far more than the uranium in it! Something is hiding in these old rocks.

PANEL 7 · CHALCOLITE MADE BY YOU (careful) → ABOUT
  GAME: Chalcolite made by you: about a third as much electricity as pure uranium.
  💭 Aha! My home-made chalcolite is just as strong as the uranium in it, no more. So the one from the mine really has something extra hiding in it!

PANEL 8 · LEAD (careful) → NONE
  GAME: Lead: no electricity at all.
  💭 Oh… a different answer this time! The first one must have been a slip. Lead is heavy, and does nothing at all. So it isn’t weight alone.

PANEL 9 · RED PHOSPHORUS (careful) → NONE
  GAME: Red phosphorus: no electricity at all.
  💭 There! Red phosphorus: nothing at all, and it’s the same element as white phosphorus! So it was never the phosphorus. The white kind is just burning slowly in the air.

YOUR PHYSICS: THE RESTLESS ATOM
  1. The energy comes from inside the heaviest atoms, and an atom that spends it turns into another element.  (panels 5, 6)
  2. Only the heaviest atoms do it, uranium and thorium, the two heaviest known: the table of elements ends where atoms start to leak.  (panels 3)
  3. Old uranium rocks are full of what uranium turned into: new elements, far stronger than uranium.  (panels 5, 6, 7)

YOUR WORLD (if it were true): landmark dome; vignettes rockclock, hospital, table118
  (1) Atoms of uranium split in power stations: a million times the energy of any fire.  ← panels 5, 6
  (2) A rock cut open is a clock: the atoms that changed in it say the Earth is four and a half billion years old.
  (3) Rays from changing atoms treat cancer.
  (4) The table of elements ends where atoms fall apart: 118, the last ones made by hand.
  In this world, as in ours, two new elements came out of tons of pitchblende within the year: polonium and radium (1898). Radium keeps itself warmer than the room (1903); its rays, fired at gold leaf, showed that the atom has a tiny, heavy heart (1911); atoms of uranium were split (1938), first for a weapon, then for power. Rocks are dated by the atoms that changed in them: the Earth is four and a half billion years old.
