| **Weil's Vase** | Weil (1940–41), *The Iliad, or The Poem of Force*, tr. Mary McCarthy, *Politics* (1945); Pendle Hill Pamphlet 91 (1956). As an Attic red-figure vase: you are the soul inside a warrior, and for twelve days Zeus's scales decide whose day it is. Strike, take, pass or raise the man before you, and whatever force you use or suffer turns you a band more to stone; the higher your pan swings, the less you can stop your own arm, and the stonier the man who stands over you when it falls. | [play](https://steffenhuck.github.io/paper-to-game/weil/weils-vase.html) · [memo](weil/memo.md) |
