How to play

Fish termites out of the mound with a stem of grass, before your mother moves on.

The long grass

Touch a stem to hold it up: it is drawn over a ruler of inches, drooping as much as it really droops, and the line under it says how long, how thick and how stiff it is. Take it puts it in one of your three slots; touch a slot to put it back. Strip the leaves pulls the leaves off a leafy twig. The mound is sixty yards away and you can't see its hole from here, so choose before you go: you won't come back.

The mound

The big drawing is the mound cut open: the hole at the top, a passage that bends twice, and termites waiting below the first few inches. The small numbers along the passage count inches in. Drag down on the drawing to push your stem in. Let go to leave it there: termites bite on to whatever comes into their passage, and they need a moment. Drag up to pull it out: if it moves faster than they can hold on, they fall off. The stem goes in until something stops it: a stem too thick for the hole, leaves catching on the rim, a floppy stem folding at a bend, or its own length.

Each dip takes one sun from the afternoon. So does watching your mother fish at her own hole, and fetching another stem from beside the mound. When the twelve suns are gone, she moves off. A stem's end bends after a few dips; Bite off the end makes it an inch shorter and straight again. When your mother throws a bent stem down, Pick up her stem takes it. Touching any of these buttons shows you, in the hand box, what it would do before you let go.

After each dip the game says what happened (how far in, whether there was a pause, how fast it came out, how many termites). Then, in red, a note from Jane Goodall: which age of young chimpanzee fishes the way you just did, and a tick on her ruler of ages.

The hollow

Rainwater in a hollow of a tree, too deep for your lips. The same drag: down to push in, up to pull out; whatever comes up wet, you suck. Your hand is one of the things you can hold. Chew the end crumples a grass end; Pick some leaves gives you a handful from the branch, which you can chew too. Five suns; the tube at the right fills with sips until you have drunk your fill.
