# Noether's Ball — note for the author

1. The final sweep (revision 6, 1 Nov 2026): no drawing anywhere. Nine cards, each a small picture with its formula on screen and a change; one answer, *it matters* or *it doesn't*; then the trip after the change beside the first, the formula's reason in one line, and the formula's number before and after. Every change that doesn't matter wins a prize, shown on a graph; the slide's energy comes out empty, and the last card says why. Six red badges for the paper, three pink for the traps; no score, just the shelf and a sentence.
2. Look first at `screenshot-a-c1-answer.png` (a prize won: energy's two parts and their flat sum), `screenshot-a-c4-answer.png` (a trap: the magnet, and why in the formula), `screenshot-a-c8-answer.png` (the empty prize: zero on the real slide and on a straight one) and `screenshot-a-end.png`.
3. Everything tunable is in the `CONFIG` block at the top of `mechanism.js`, the only copy of the mechanism: the six worlds' numbers, the finite changes shown, the thresholds. The cards' words and badges are in the same file. Edit, run `node calibration.js` (12 checks against the paper), then `python3 build.py`, which refuses to finish if the embedded mechanism differs from the file; `node session-test.js` plays the game through at phone width.
4. Least sure about: whether card 9's reveal, the longest text in the game, is too much at once; and whether the slide's formula in words ("distance ÷ √drop") lands with a stranger.

Builder's review only: the screens were checked from screenshots and a scripted session at phone width, not by a stranger.
