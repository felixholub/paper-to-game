| **Jevons's Noon** | Jevons (1866), *The Coal Question*, second edition: preface, chapters I, VII and XVIII, and the frontispiece. On Jevons's own ruled paper, set the pace of Britain's coal for ten generations on a ruler that reads both ways, how big the fire gets and how long the coal lasts, and watch it come up out of the ground beneath the line, square by square. New engines come in when they pay, and the fire climbs over your line; hold it level, and the young go over the sea; America passes either way. A ledger keeps what each generation got, in income per head and the days of earnings that fuel took, with a box for the generations after 2065; the verdict scores the typical generation against the best path found, and reads how high, how long, and who got the coal. Then the curve continued past 1865 to today. | [play](https://steffenhuck.github.io/paper-to-game/jevons/jevons-noon.html) · [memo](jevons/memo.md) |
