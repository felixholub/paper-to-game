| **Foot's Bad Man** | Foot (1967), *Oxford Review* 5:5–15. The problem of abortion and the doctrine of the double effect, the paper in which the runaway tram first appears, as a silent-film melodrama in three reels. You are the bad man: spring her cases (the tram with its brakes cut, the bargain, the medicine, the brew, the beggar, the cave) on three good people who each live by a rule, say what you would do in their chair, and watch the audience judge them. Counting lives falls to the bargain; the doctrine of the double effect falls to the brew, Foot's gas case, where the death is only a side effect; Foot's own line, never harm one to help others, gives the bad man nothing. At the end you learn which rule you chose like, and an epilogue plays the three cases about abortion the paper was written for. | [play](https://steffenhuck.github.io/paper-to-game/foot/foots-bad-man.html) · [memo](foot/memo.md) |
