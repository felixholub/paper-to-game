a END: Night 7. The room is light.
  The heaviness lay on the room four times (eight beats in all). In it you stayed at the cross seven times, went to the window not once, and looked within one time. At the cross you saw the garland, the drops, the face, the scourging, the laugh, the drying, the question, the side, His mother and "It is I". Within, you asked what brought the heaviness. The cheer changed for you three times, with no time between.
  It came after the cross four times: after one thing, as it would have come after any other. She checked the same: "I sinned not wherefore I should be left to myself; also I deserved not to have this blessed feeling." The blanket never changed: "He keepeth us even alike secure in woe and in weal." And: "it is not God’s will that we follow the feelings of pain in sorrow and mourning for them, but that we suddenly pass over."
  The dark that comes over you is not a message about you. Between the dark and the light there is no time. What you saw, you saw by staying; what you sought and did not see counted the same.
