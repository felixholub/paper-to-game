# Who Started It? — note for the authors

1. Look first at `screenshot-a-clash3-after1.png` and `screenshot-a-clash3-end.png` (a clash started on purpose by a side with a popular cause: hold out, the crowd gathers under them and costs you, you give way, the seam is overprinted and stitched: your hybrid cluster as a move), then `screenshot-a-clash2-end.png` (the malign one: the pages given are lost, the crowd sits with you, it tears) and `screenshot-a-end.png` (the player's nine beside your 77 in three heaps). Then play it once holding out against everything, and once paying everyone halfway.
2. Everything tunable is in `CONFIG` at the top of `mechanism.js`, the only copy of the mechanism: your counts from Table 1 and Figure 4, each tagged; the deck (4 : 3 : 2, your clusters' 35 : 26 : 16 in nine); the prices and the purse (all ours); the crowd and what it costs (ours, generalised from the IRENA case); the years-later rates (Figure 4 and note 10). The fourteen made-up clashes and every line the game says are in the same file (`POOL`, `PAPER_WORD`, `words`, `verdict`). Edit, run `node calibration.js` (48 checks, including your counts reproduced by a player who behaves as your actors do), then `python3 build.py`, which refuses to build if the embedded mechanism differs from the file.
3. What we were least sure about: (a) the crowd: the game runs the whole hybrid path through public opinion turning against the challenged body, which your paper offers as a first insight from one case and calls an open question; your spheres finding (hybrid conflicts mostly cross spheres of authority) is not played; (b) the made-up clashes, which borrow the shapes of real ones (patents and epidemics, a landmine ban, a trade court, a regional bloc and a council) and may sit closer to or further from your codes than we can tell without the dataset; (c) two places where your text and your figures disagree, which we resolved toward the figures: the conclusion's "two-thirds malign, one-third hybrid" against Figure 4's 16 and 26, and Table 1's counts for interests and across-spheres, which look swapped against Figure 6; (d) whether you would rather the accidents could also fail for "idiosyncratic reasons" (2 of your 35), which we dropped.

This game has had the builder's review only: screenshots and scripted browser sessions at 380 px. It has not been played by real strangers, and its length is an estimate.
