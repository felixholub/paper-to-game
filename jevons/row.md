| **Jevons's Noon** | Jevons (1866), *The Coal Question*, second edition: preface, chapters I, VII and XVIII, and the frontispiece. On Jevons's own ruled paper, set the pace of Britain's coal for six generations, from a stand to faster than ever, and watch it come up out of the ground beneath the line, square by square. Fit the new engine to make the coal last, and see the fire climb over your line; hold it level, and the young go over the sea; America passes either way. Then your noon, your evening, and the curve continued past 1865 to today. | [play](https://steffenhuck.github.io/paper-to-game/jevons/jevons-noon.html) · [memo](jevons/memo.md) |
