How to play

The machine

Lovelace's machine never thinks. It reads a chain of cards from the top, one at a time, and does what each card says. Then it stops.

Hanging cards

Tap a card at the bottom to hang it at the end of your chain. Tap a card on the chain to take it off. Then tap Run the loom and watch.

The order

The dashed squares show the pattern that was ordered. Each stitch the loom weaves fills one square. If a stitch is wrong, it gets a black frame.

The wheel and Go back

The wheel says how many rounds are ordered. The Go back card sends the machine back to the top of the chain. Each time, the wheel clicks down one. When it shows 0, Go back does nothing, and the machine carries on to the end.

The switch

On the fourth loom, the same cards are read as threads, as musical notes, or as sums. A card is just holes: what they mean is up to whoever sets the machine.
