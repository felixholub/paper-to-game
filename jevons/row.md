| **Jevons's Noon** | Jevons (1866), *The Coal Question*, second edition: preface, chapters I, VII and XVIII, and the frontispiece. On Jevons's own ruled paper, set the pace of Britain's coal for six generations on a ruler that reads both ways, how big the fire gets and how long the coal lasts, and watch it come up out of the ground beneath the line, square by square. Fit the new engine to make the coal last, and see the fire climb over your line; hold it level, and the young go over the sea; America passes either way. The sheet runs on to 2065, and the verdict reads how high and how long. Then the curve continued past 1865 to today. | [play](https://steffenhuck.github.io/paper-to-game/jevons/jevons-noon.html) · [memo](jevons/memo.md) |
