| **Julian's Window** | Julian of Norwich (1373/c. 1393), *Revelations of Divine Love*, tr. Grace Warrack (1901), chapters i–xxxii. As a shōjo manga: you are Julian in the bed, one night in a cell with one window, a cross on the wall. Sixteen times, choose where to look — the cross (the next sight of the Passion, drawn in ink, sweet in the sureness and dreadful in the heaviness), the window (the sky and the hour; nothing shown, nothing harmful), or within (the hazelnut, what sin is, what brought this heaviness, how all might be well, and one question that meets a wall). A heaviness comes over the page and goes again on its own clock, about half the beats; it follows nothing you do, the rose blanket never changes, and "Where is now any point of thy pain?" comes only to the one at the cross when it turns, with no time between. No score; a verdict in words, and her sentence. | [play](https://steffenhuck.github.io/paper-to-game/julian/julians-window.html) · [memo](julian/memo.md) |
