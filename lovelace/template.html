How to play

The machine

Lovelace's machine never thinks. It reads your chain of cards from the top, one card at a time, and does what each card says. When it runs out of cards, it stops.

Hanging cards

Tap a card at the bottom to hang it at the end of your chain. Tap × to take a card off. Only so many cards fit. Then tap Run the loom and watch. The dashed squares are the order; each stitch the loom weaves fills one. A wrong stitch gets a black frame.

Go back

A Go back card sends the machine back up the chain, to the card it points at. You choose that card on the Go back card itself (“to card 1”, “to card 2” …). The arc on the left shows where it jumps.

The wheels

Every Go back card belongs to a wheel. On each Go back card you set, with − and +, how many times its loop is woven; its wheel keeps count. Each time the machine reaches Go back, the wheel clicks down one. When it reaches 0, the machine does not go back: it carries on down the chain, and the wheel springs back to its number, ready for next time. That is how one loop can sit inside another.

Fewest cards

When the bell rings you are told the fewest cards that can do the job. On the hard looms there is no spare room at all.

Stuck?

After three runs that don't ring the bell, you can ask to see a way. It's one way, not the only one.
