How to play

The machine

Lovelace's machine never thinks. It reads your chain of cards from the top, one card at a time, and does what each card says. When it runs out of cards, it stops.

Hanging cards

Tap a card at the bottom to hang it at the end of your chain. Tap × to take a card off. Only so many cards fit. Then tap Run the loom and watch. The dashed squares are the order; each stitch the loom weaves fills one. A wrong stitch gets a black frame.

Go back

The machine counts with wheels: numbers inside it. Every wheel starts at 0. A Set card puts a number on a wheel (you choose it on the card, with − and +), whatever it showed before. That is the only way a wheel gets a number.

A Go back card takes 1 off its wheel and looks at the result. If the wheel isn't 0 yet, the machine goes back up the chain to the card you chose on the Go back card (“card 1”, “card 2” …); the arc on the left shows the jump. If the wheel shows 0, the machine carries on down the chain.

Set cards

A wheel that reaches 0 stays at 0 until a Set card puts a number on it again. That is what a loop inside a loop needs: the inner wheel must be set again on every round of the outer loop, so the outer Go back has to send the machine back to (or above) the inner wheel's Set card. A Go back card can jump to any card above it, a Set card included.

Fewest cards

When the bell rings you are told the fewest cards that can do the job. On the hard looms there is no spare room at all.

Stuck?

After three runs that don't ring the bell, you can ask to see a way. It's one way, not the only one. Every loom can be tried again after the bell, and the end screen takes you back into any of them; your best go is the one that counts.
