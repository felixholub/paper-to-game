| **Augustine's Clock** | Augustine (c. 397–400), *Confessions* XI, with X on memory, tr. Pusey (1838). What is time? As a Bauhaus poster: a voice sounds and stops, and you hold a black circle as long as it lasted, with no clock but your mind, because nothing on screen grows while it lasts. A potter's wheel turns (and is kicked when you press), the sun climbs (and stands still), a hymn's long syllable is laid against a short one that stopped before it began, and the first voice is fetched back from memory at the end. | [play](https://steffenhuck.github.io/paper-to-game/augustine/augustines-clock.html) · [memo](augustine/memo.md) |
