THE LAST PAGE
 By the end of the afternoon you fished like a grown-up chimpanzee, which took the infants Goodall watched about two years; then you made a sponge out of a termite stem.
  You caught 54 termites this afternoon; your mother caught about 70.
  Your first dip looked like an infant’s of three to four (a floppy stem folding at the first bend); your last, like a grown-up’s (a stiff stem, deep in, a pause, out slowly).
  You watched your mother once and tried the rest yourself, and you fished with the stem she threw away.
  At the hollow you pushed a grass stem in the way you fish for termites; chewing and sucking the end crumpled it into a sponge, and you drank your fill.
