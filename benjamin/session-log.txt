== VERDICT: MANY SAW THE FACE, MOST OF THEM IN COPIES, AND THE HALO WENT OUT WITH THEM. | Its halo is gone. It went out to the people: to the faithful on the day, in woodcuts, in lithographs, in photographs and on film.
   You kept the veil down through the cave, then lifted it in the temple and put the face in woodcuts in the cathedral. The faithful had seen the face itself on the day; from the cathedral on, the face was in the people's hands, in copies, each carrying a trace of the halo and each spending some of the original's.
   The halo was never in the face alone. It was between the face and the people: the one place, the one time, the distance that was kept. No copy carries it whole, and the age sets what the people can see. This is exhibition value, which the age of copying puts in cult value's place. The copy meets the people halfway, in their own rooms, and what it brings them is not the halo but the face. Benjamin does not mourn it: the halo was the mark of a distance, and the distance is gone. What comes next is on his last page.
   The halo is gone; the longing for it is not. Where that longing is served, Benjamin says what comes of it: the collector's certificate on a face nobody sees; the film star's "personality", a halo rebuilt to be sold; and the leader's face on every wall and over a square full of people, which is politics made beautiful, and it ends in war. "Let art be made, though the world perish."
   A second run is a different fate: open earlier, open later, or never.
