# Weil's Vase — note for the author

1. Look first at `screenshot-a-day2-result.png` (the man of stone struck, and stone climbs to your own knees: the double edge, said at the moment it happens), then `screenshot-b-day3-creep.png` and `screenshot-b-day7-nointerval.png` (the interval going as the pan rises and the soul goes), `screenshot-b-day8-down.png` (the hard player under a man as stony as his own swing), and `screenshot-a-day12-result.png` (the night). Then play it once striking everything and once sparing everyone.
2. Everything tunable is in `CONFIG` at the top of `mechanism.js`, the only copy of the mechanism: Weil's two numbers (prestige three quarters of force, p. 19; Lycaon's twelve days, p. 24), and the rest tagged "ours". The seesaw's clock, swing and hold; what each gesture pushes; the man over you (6 − 1.5 × the swing); the armed men's thresholds; the interval's timer; the deck of men. Every line the game says is in the same file (`MEN`, `sceneLine`, `words`, `verdict`). Edit, run `node calibration.js` (30 checks, including the essay's patterns under 18 shifts of our numbers), then `python3 build.py`, which refuses to build if the embedded mechanism differs from the file. The drawing is in `rig.js` (display only).
3. What we were least sure about: (a) the rule that every act of force, used or suffered, costs exactly one line: it makes "to the same degree" literal, and it is a rule, not a finding. (b) The man over you being as stony as the scales are high: it makes the geometric retribution visible, but ties one man's deafness to one swing, which Weil does not. (c) Moderation's peril: we made a half-stone man strike a player whose prestige has sunk. Weil's peril may be larger than our toy lets it be; a careful player keeps most of his soul in two lives of three. (d) Whether the night's grace should leave a line, as it does, or only flash the whole figure and fade back, as "brief duration" might ask. (e) The made-up men and their lines (Thersites as "a soldier of your own army", Chryses as "an old priest"); the quotations are McCarthy's.

This game has had the builder's review only: screenshots and scripted browser sessions at 380 px. It has not been played by real strangers, and its length is an estimate.
