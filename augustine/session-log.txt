  SCALES: Tonight you measured on every kind of scale a mind has. The moment of joy had none: a length with nothing to lay it against. Three things from yesterday had only an order, longer and shorter. Your night and your day had a ratio. Your betweens had equal steps, and they were not the clock’s: equal steps in you are equal ratios outside, and on such a scale nothing lies infinitely far off. And twice you touched the zero: once to find that the low one cannot be counted in nothings, once to take half of a voice from it. The zero is the present, which has no length and is where all of this was measured; and it is the zero Augustine says the world began from, when there was no then.
