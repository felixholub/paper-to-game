== VERDICT
   Many saw the face, most of them in copies, and the halo went out with them.
   What you did: You kept the veil down through the cave, then lifted it in the temple and put the face in lithographs in the print shop. The faithful had seen the face itself on the day; from the print shop on, the face was in the people's hands, in copies, each carrying a trace of the halo and each spending some of the original's.
   What Benjamin says: The halo was never in the face alone. It was between the face and the people: the one place, the one time, the distance that was kept. No copy carries it whole, and the age sets what the people can see. This is exhibition value, which the age of copying puts in cult value's place. The copy meets the people halfway, in their own rooms, and what it brings them is not the halo but the face. Benjamin does not mourn it: the halo was the mark of a distance, and the distance is gone. What comes next is on his last page.
   And then, 1936: The halo is gone; the longing for it is not. Where that longing is served, Benjamin says what comes of it: the collector's certificate on a face nobody sees; the film star's "personality", a halo rebuilt to be sold; and the leader's face on every wall and over a square full of people, which is politics made beautiful, and it ends in war. "Let art be made, though the world perish."
